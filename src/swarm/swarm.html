Formation

Speed is how fast each bot travels along the shape (0 = still). Random changes shape, and scatter re-draws, every "shuffle" seconds.

Nuker

ClearBox: the box is cut into 8x8 columns; each bot takes the nearest free one and clears it top down - no two bots on one. Sphere: everything in reach of each bot, wherever it is. Your controller's nuker (its own box strips) takes over while it has the nuker on.

Printer

Prints what your controller has placed in Litematica - live from its instance, else Litematica's saved file (written when it leaves the world). The schematic file comes from the folder below, else where Litematica found it. The build is cut into 8x8 columns; each bot prints the nearest free one bottom up. Materials come from the controller printer's supply chests (and its map-art colour chests). Settings default to the controller's printer; set here, they win.

Combat

Friends, your own accounts and the swarm's bots are never attacked. Your controller's Defend / attack target apply too, to bots that listen to it.

Food & chests

No food chest: bots eat what they carry. Tools: what they carry, else a signed tools chest nearby. Storage is only used while collecting.

Ender chest link

Every bot links whenever it is not printing: an ender chest it carries is placed first, else the nearest in any bot's loaded chunks or remembered within the range, else one bot flies a spiral out to the range looking. Then back to where it works. Tools and weapons are swapped into the held slot and back after 5 s unused; a totem goes in an empty offhand and armor on empty slots, kept on while it works. Food comes out when it runs low. A chest trip breaks the link and it links again; print jobs put everything back and never use it.
